    Mahatma Gandhi was a key leader in India’s struggle for independence, known for his strong belief in truth, non-violence, and simple living. He inspired millions by leading movements like the Non-Cooperation Movement, Civil Disobedience Movement, and the Quit India Movement, proving that peaceful resistance can bring massive change. Gandhi worked to remove social evils such as untouchability and promoted unity among all communities. His principles of honesty, courage, and non-violence continue to influence people around the world even today.
